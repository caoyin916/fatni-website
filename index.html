Our Story

ABOUT FAT NI BBQ

Fat Ni BBQ (胖妮烧烤) brings the bold, smoky tradition of Chinese charcoal-grill BBQ to the heart of DFW. From cumin-dusted lamb skewers to charcoal-kissed scallops and fresh squid, every skewer is grilled to order over real charcoal — the way it's done on the streets of northern China.

Pull up a seat at our sauce bar, grab a cold Tsingtao, and enjoy the kind of late-night communal feast that makes the best memories. With two locations in Plano and Carrollton, Fat Ni is your spot for authentic Chinese BBQ in North Texas.
